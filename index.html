01

Field Engineering

As a Field Engineer I work close to the hardware, the network, and the people who depend on them. My job is to diagnose issues on site, keep infrastructure healthy, and turn chaos into something stable and documented.

That hands-on mindset — listen, isolate, fix, verify — is the foundation I bring to every technical problem, whether it's a broken deployment or a flaky device in the field.

02

Building iOS Apps

When I'm not on site, I'm building iOS apps. Swift and SwiftUI let me move from idea to something you can tap in a weekend — and that's addictive.

Shipping small apps teaches product sense, UI craft, and the discipline of finishing. Each project is practice for bigger products and for leading teams that build them.

03

Toward Management

My career path points toward management. I want to grow from solving tickets and shipping features into leading people — setting direction, unblocking teams, and raising the bar on quality.

Field experience keeps me grounded in reality; building apps keeps me close to product. Together they are the base for becoming a manager who still understands the work.
